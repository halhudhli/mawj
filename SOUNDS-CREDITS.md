# Mawj sounds

The sea sounds are made from CC0 (public-domain) recordings, cleaned and mixed for Mawj:

- Breaking waves: "beach ocean waves" by jasinski (freesound 18363), via OpenGameArt (qubodup), CC0.
- Calm sea: BigSoundBank, Denis Chardonnet (sounds 0266 and 0267), CC0.

The gold notes and the UI tones are synthesised for Mawj.
